## 2. Research & Foundational Papers
- **INSEAD Executive Strategy**:
  - [What's Your Personal Social Media Strategy? - INSEAD](https://www.insead.edu/faculty-research/publications/journal-articles/whats-your-personal-social-media-strategy)
  - Core insight: Executive branding is not one-size-fits-all. It maps into strategic archetypes (e.g., Megaphone/Broadcaster, Magnet/Attractor, Networker/Connector, Monitor/Sensing) based on business goals.

## 4. Personal Branding & Content Execution Frameworks
- **Justin Welsh's Content Operating System**:
  - The "Hub and Spoke" content architecture.
  - Turning 1 core insight into 4 distinct formats: The Observation, The Contrarian Take, The Playbook / Teardown, The Vulnerable Lesson.
- **Sahil Bloom's Curiosity Architecture**:
  - Hook anatomy: Contrast, Curiosity Gap, Quantified Transformation, Direct Callout.
- **Amanda Natividad's Permissionless Apprentice**:
  - High-agency building in public: Documenting bugs, failed iterations, and architectural trade-offs to build organic investor and candidate trust.
- **Paul Graham's Essay Principles**:
  - Clear, unpretentious prose without corporate jargon or buzzwords.

## 5. Anti-AI Linguistic Filter (The "Anti-Slop" Engine)
- **Banned AI Cliches**:
  - *"In today's fast-paced digital world..."*
  - *"Delve into..."*
  - *"Game-changer"* / *"Revolutionizing"*
  - *"Thrilled to announce..."* / *"Humbled and honored..."*
  - Mandatory emoji spam (🚀💡🔥📈)
- **Authentic Founder Voice Rules**:
  - Short, punchy cadence.
  - Concrete nouns and real numbers ("shaved 340ms of p99 latency", "talked to 47 retail store managers").
  - Admitting what broke and what was learned.
  - Active voice with clear ownership ("We decided to kill the feature", not "A decision was made").
