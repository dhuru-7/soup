## 1. Problem Context & The Founder Dilemma
Founders know that in 2026, founder-led visibility is the highest-ROI lever for fundraising, hiring top 1% talent, and acquiring early customers. However:
1. **Time Poverty**: Founders work 80+ hours/week running operations and cannot spend 2 hours a day drafting social media posts.
2. **The "AI Slop" Trap**: Generic ChatGPT prompts yield cringe-inducing, robotic buzzwords ("Delve into", "Humbled & honored", rocket emojis) that destroy credibility.
3. **Lack of Execution System**: One-off posts do not compound. Founders need a strategy tied directly to measurable 3-to-6-month business milestones.

---

## 2. Mandatory Core Requirements (What We Must Build)

### Module 1: Founder Onboarding & DNA Extraction
- Captures Founder Background & Origin Story (unfair advantages, past failures, technical or operational pedigree).
- Domain & Industry (DeepTech, AI Infrastructure, D2C, FinTech, B2B SaaS, HealthTech).
- Target Audience (VCs, Devs/Engineers, Enterprise Buyers, Direct Consumers).
- Tone Archetype & Voiceprint (The Contrarian Architect, The Vulnerable Operator, The Visionary Storyteller, The Technical Teardown specialist).
- Preferred Platforms (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Substack, YouTube Shorts).
- 3-Month and 6-Month Business Objectives (e.g., Raise $2M Seed, Hire 3 Lead AI Engineers, Reach $50k MRR, Book 5 Podcast/Keynote appearances).

### Module 2: Anti-AI Content Positioning & Topic Strategy
- **Anti-AI Filter**: Strict ban on algorithmic cliches, synthetic fluff, and passive corporate speak.
- **Voiceprint Calibration**: Content reflecting personal stakes, real numerical metrics, specific trade-offs, and contrarian perspectives.
- **Strategic Content Pillars**: e.g.,
  - *Pillar 1 (60%)*: "Building in Public & Technical Truths" (high proof of competence).
  - *Pillar 2 (25%)*: "Industry Contrarianism & Mental Models" (thought leadership).
  - *Pillar 3 (15%)*: "Founder Vulnerability & Culture/Hiring" (human connection).

### Module 3: Practical Weekly Content Calendar
- 7-day actionable schedule with:
  - High-converting hooks tailored to platform typography and line breaks.
  - Draft structures (Hook $\rightarrow$ Friction/Conflict $\rightarrow$ Core Data/Insight $\rightarrow$ Key Takeaway $\rightarrow$ Discussion Trigger).
  - Visual Direction & Asset Brief (exact guidance on photo/video/diagram to accompany the post).
  - Follow-up Actions (community engagement checklist, DM outreach triggers, comment pin strategies).

### Module 4: Raw Asset / Brain Dump Ingestion Pipeline
- Real founder inputs are messy: voice memos, WhatsApp screenshots, raw whiteboard photos, 2-line Slack wins, or rough opinions.
- System ingests these raw assets and transforms them into 3 distinct post angles with custom visual guidance without losing the raw, authentic edge.

### Module 5: Progress & Authority Tracking
- Tracks tangible ROI against the founder's initial 3- or 6-month goals:
  - Inbound Leads & Customer Inquiries.
  - Talent / Recruiting Pipeline (inbound candidate messages).
  - Speaking & Podcast Opportunities.
  - Founder Authority Score & Visibility Velocity.

---

## 3. Strict Boundary Constraints (What NOT to Build)
- **DO NOT build only a caption generator!**
- Isolated prompt boxes that simply spit out single captions without strategy, calendar, voiceprint, or goals will fail the judging criteria.
- It must be an **end-to-end execution system**.

## 4. Expected Prototype Demonstration
- **MANDATORY**: Show **two completely different founder profiles** receiving distinct strategies, calendars, and content recommendations from the *same platform*.
- **Demo Profile 1**: Technical AI SaaS Founder (Focus: Technical authority, developer trust, hiring engineers, GitHub stars).
- **Demo Profile 2**: D2C Sustainable Consumer Founder (Focus: Retail expansion, consumer loyalty, brand storytelling, angel investment).
